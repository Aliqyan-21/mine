## Experiments

### [fatrat](https://github.com/aliqyan-21/fatrat)
An attempt at a microbenchmarking tool/experiment.

it tries to tell you when:
- your code got optimized away
- your environment is too noisy
- your numbers don’t really mean much
> good tools handle noise mathematically. fatrat handles it philosophically.
