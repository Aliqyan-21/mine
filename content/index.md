# Aliqyan Abid

Hello, and welcome, a site made by rattip, my personal tool btw, [know more](#home)

I write code the way I think, close to the metal, suspicious of that mf abstraction I haven't earned yet.

> software is the craft where `I` can build the tools `I` build with!

I'm drawn to systems, because understanding them makes everything else make sense. I want to know
**why** the hell things work in the way it does (crazy shit when u know it).

I build in the open because closed work dies with your hard drive.

Most of what I make is unfinished (it's part of my nature I guess). I've made peace with that.

## what I believe

- Complexity is usually a failure of design, not a requirement of the problem.
- Open source is an act of faith in strangers.
- Write tool for yourselves, they become the best like `vim`.

## what this place is

Writing about things I'm figuring out. Projects mid-tinkering. Opinions I'll probably revise. Technical and non-technical slop.
